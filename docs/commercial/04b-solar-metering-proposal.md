## 1. Executive Summary & Owner Confirmed Rules

In commercial station mode, power is generated from two sources joining the common station bus: diesel generators and a commercial solar photovoltaic (PV) plant.

### Owner Binding Rules (Confirmed in C4.1)
1. **Independent Solar Production Meter:** The solar plant has its own independent cumulative energy meter measuring total solar production (kWh). This meter is captured at daily closing under the **Tier-2 workflow** (requires supervisor approval before becoming official).
2. **Dedicated Solar Feeder Meter:** The solar plant also has its own dedicated feeder with an energy meter for power delivered to the network. It is treated as an ordinary feeder meter (`FeederEnergySent`), summed directly with generator feeders into total "energy sent to the grid".
3. **Dynamic Maintenance Oil-Set Configuration (for C6):** The oil item and quantity for oil-set replacement are NOT known yet and must never be hard-coded. The owner will configure them in oil stock settings. If a generator's service definition lacks a configured oil item/quantity, the replacement workflow must display a prominent warning that stock was not decremented, and must never skip silently.
4. **Permit Numbering Prefix (for C6):** Approved format is `{StationCode}-WP-{seq:D5}`.
5. **Parked Item:** The theoretical "diesel-saved estimate" (estimating diesel liters saved by solar kWh) remains strictly **PARKED** for future phases and will not be implemented now.

   {
       GeneratorEnergyProduced = 0,

### (d) Energy Balance Formulation

The commercial station energy balance will be calculated strictly as:

$$\text{Total Production} = \sum \Delta \text{GeneratorEnergyProduced} + \Delta \text{SolarEnergyProduced}$$

$$\text{Total Sent to Grid} = \sum \Delta \text{FeederEnergySent} \quad (\text{Sum of all active feeders, including the solar feeder})$$

$$\text{Loss (Unexplained)} = \text{Total Production} - \text{Total Sent to Grid} - \text{Internal Consumption (if metered)}$$

$$\text{Loss Percentage} = \begin{cases} \dfrac{\text{Loss}}{\text{Total Production}} \times 100 & \text{if Total Production} > 0 \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

$$\text{Solar Share (\%)} = \begin{cases} \dfrac{\Delta \text{SolarEnergyProduced}}{\text{Total Production}} \times 100 & \text{if Total Production} > 0 \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

> **Key Rule Confirmed by Owner:** Energy from solar enters the station bus and exports through feeders. Both generator feeders and the solar feeder are summed into "Total Sent to Grid". The difference between Total Production (Diesel + Solar) and Total Exported is the station network loss.
